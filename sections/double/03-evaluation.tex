\subsection{Research questions and subjects}
Tie each question to a capability: effectiveness measures the target
behavior, overhead measures resources, and an ablation isolates a design
decision's contribution.

Describe subject selection, version pinning, exclusions, and the unit of
analysis. Repeated inputs from one model are not automatically independent
subjects. Identify the population the conclusions concern.

\subsection{Baselines, metrics, and controls}
Use strong baselines and match budgets. Define every denominator, including
the negative population for a false-positive rate. Explain labeling,
deduplication, and how distinct root causes were determined.

Report workload shape, concurrency, warm-up, repetition, and hardware.
Distinguish throughput from latency. Record model checkpoints,
preprocessing, and precision; introduce learning concepts only as needed
\citep{goodfellow2016}.
