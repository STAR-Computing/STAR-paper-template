STAR LAB IDENTITY

STAR = System Trustworthiness and AI Reliability.

The sculpted S uses two balanced strokes, rotated 180 degrees around an
open four-point star. The same star appears inside the custom A in STAR.
Every letter in STAR LAB is custom vector geometry. Matching stroke weights,
angled S/T terminals, and optical spacing connect the wordmark to the symbol.
The restrained sans-serif typography coordinates with the text-only GMU name.
The full lab name appears beneath the logo row in both paper layouts.

Files
  star-mark.*          Standard emblem
  star-mark-small.*    Wider openings for 16-32 pixel uses
  star-lockup.*        Emblem and custom STAR LAB lettering
  star-signature.*     Full signature including the expanded lab name
  *-mono.*             Solid dark artwork for one-color reproduction
  *-reverse.*          White-and-cyan artwork for dark backgrounds
  star-icon-N.png      Transparent icons: 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256 pixels
  star-favicon.ico     Multi-resolution 16/32/48-pixel website favicon

Formats
  PDF              Vector artwork for LaTeX and print
  SVG              Outlined vector artwork for editing and web use
  PNG              High-resolution transparent artwork

Colors
  Midnight blue    #25356B
  Cyan             #00A8C6
  Monochrome       #202020

Preserve aspect ratio and leave clear space of at least one quarter of the
mark height. Use the small-use emblem at 16-32 pixels. Use the compact lockup
in paper headers; reserve the full-name signature for larger uses where the
descriptor is readable (approximately 480 pixels wide or more). All STAR SVG and PDF lettering
is outlined; no fonts or external tools are required to use these assets.
The template displays a separate GEORGE MASON UNIVERSITY text wordmark.
The optional official GMU image and its provenance are in institutions/,
not in the STAR logo kit. See DESIGN.txt for the concept-generation brief.

Rebuild STAR artwork with scripts/make-logo.py. Ordinary LaTeX builds use
the included PDFs directly. Previews: output/STAR-logo-preview.png and
output/STAR-identity-preview.png. The approved earlier design is archived
in build/identity-polish/previous/.
