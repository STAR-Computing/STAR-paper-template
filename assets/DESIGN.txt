STAR LAB - REFINED S-AND-STAR IDENTITY

Lab name: System Trustworthiness and AI Reliability (STAR).

Concept exploration: OpenAI built-in image_gen tool, logo-brand mode.
No API/CLI fallback was used. Three concepts were explored on one board;
the first S-shaped concept was selected and reconstructed as vector paths.
Final production files are generated by scripts/make-logo.py. The glyphs
are outlined; no fonts or image-generation service are needed by Overleaf.

Design brief / prompt summary:
Create a premium identity for STAR LAB, researching artificial intelligence,
software engineering, systems security, and ML systems. Explore a simple
S monogram with two opposing continuous rounded strokes and a small
four-point star in negative space. Use refined sans-serif STAR LAB lettering,
midnight navy #25356B and cyan #00A8C6, flat vector-style fills, generous white
space, and excellent legibility at small publication-header sizes. Avoid
five-point stars, fragmented facets, shields, brains, circuit-board clutter,
padlocks, gradients, shadows, and esports-style lettering.

Final layout refinement:
Display the full lab name beneath the logo row, with the paper-specific
research area recorded separately in PDF metadata.
Use a two-line GEORGE MASON UNIVERSITY text treatment beside the lab mark,
with similar typography and optical size. Keep a replaceable third slot for
a collaborating institution. Preserve the original full GMU logo as an
optional asset. The text treatment is custom typesetting, not official GMU
logo artwork. All default branding is hidden in anonymous mode.

Production variants:
Color, single-ink monochrome, and white/cyan reverse. SVG/PDF are vector
outlines; PNGs have transparent backgrounds. STAR-logo-preview.png uses
white for convenient viewing.
