STAR LAB IDENTITY

STAR = System Trustworthiness and AI Reliability.

The mark is a four-point Polaris, the North Star people trust to navigate
by, split down the middle. The lavender half carries a small neural network
(learning); the light-blue half carries a code glyph (programs and proofs).
A shield holding a glowing purple sparkle sits at the center, and an aqua
orbit with three chevrons sweeps around the star. The wordmark is STAR in
bold indigo and LAB in aqua; the full lab name appears beneath the logo row
in both paper layouts.

Files
  star-mark.*          Standard emblem
  star-mark-small.*    The same emblem in light files for icons and web headers
  star-lockup.*        Emblem and STAR LAB lettering
  star-signature.*     Full signature including the expanded lab name
  *-mono.*             Grayscale artwork for one-color reproduction
  *-reverse.*          Light-outline artwork for dark backgrounds
  star-icon-N.png      Transparent icons: 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256 pixels
  star-favicon.ico     Multi-resolution 16/32/48-pixel website favicon
  star-github-avatar.png  1024-pixel square avatar, safe in a circular crop

Formats
  PDF              For LaTeX and print; the mark is embedded at high resolution
  SVG              For the web; the mark is embedded at high resolution
  PNG              High-resolution transparent artwork

Colors
  Indigo           #1A1650   outline, STAR, LaTeX STARPrimary
  Aqua             #0A8FE6   orbit, LAB, LaTeX STARAccent
  Lavender         #E9E4FB   neural half
  Light blue       #F0F8FF to #9BCEFC   symbolic half
  Shield           #3D2FA8 to #1F1866, with a #B66CFF to white sparkle

Preserve aspect ratio and leave clear space of at least one quarter of the
mark height. Use the compact lockup in paper headers; reserve the full-name
signature for larger uses where the descriptor is readable (approximately
480 pixels wide or more). All lettering is outlined; no fonts or external
tools are required to use these assets.
The template displays a separate GEORGE MASON UNIVERSITY text wordmark.
The optional official GMU image and its provenance are in institutions/,
not in the STAR logo kit. See DESIGN.txt for the design history.

The master artwork and its generator (make-logo.py) live in the lab's logo/
folder; rebuild there and copy the star-* files here. Ordinary LaTeX builds
use the included PDFs directly. Previews: output/STAR-logo-preview.png and
output/STAR-identity-preview.png.
