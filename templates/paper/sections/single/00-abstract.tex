\begin{abstract}
Learning-based software is evaluated as a model but deployed as a system.
This template demonstrates how a STAR Lab paper can connect these two
perspectives through a precise problem statement, an explicit method, and
an evaluation tied to measurable claims. It includes mathematical notation,
a vector overview, pseudocode, a results-table skeleton, citations, and an
appendix. The typography and restrained visual identity are designed for
long-form AI and machine-learning manuscripts. All prose is instructional
sample content; no experiments or empirical findings are reported. Replace
the title, authors, sections, and bibliography with the paper's own material.
\end{abstract}
